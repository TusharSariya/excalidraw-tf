The automatic readability gate failed, its implementation tests a different statistic, most “gates” cannot fail CI, the disjoint validation rule was abandoned after failure, and the view defaults to an arm materially worse than the reviewed bundle. This requires a contract/gate repair before treating OD-15, the finalize flake, or S7/M3 as the only remaining roadmap items.
