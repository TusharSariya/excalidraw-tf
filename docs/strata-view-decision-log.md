## Part I — Inherited decisions (pivot → rounds 1–7)

**Engine shape & substrate**

- **SDEC-1 · 2026-06-23→07-04 · Build a NEW engine (rcll-v2 → Strata); do not fix RCLL in place.** RCLL's host is per-container recursion (Sander's documented anti-pattern) with a readability ceiling from the cyclic-provider dense-rank collapse; the debt is the toggle/config surface. Rejected: stay-and-prune (keeps the exoskeleton). *(pivot memo; assessment §2.2/§2.5; architecture-decision Options A–B; v2.0 D2′)*
- **SDEC-2 · 2026-07-04 · The v2 substrate (depth-pinned X = `columnX[rank]` + skyline Y) is the base.** Empirical: the Q2 7-arm A/B found **no rcll arm beats plain v2** on deviation/crossings/area — confirmed on a second preset in round 6 (the substrate decision is two-preset supported). Rejected empirically: "harden rcll instead". *(round-5 §4.2; v2.0 §0.7/§4; round-6 F3)*
- **SDEC-3 · rounds 1–3 · Global leaf ranking; hulls derive [min,max] column spans bottom-up and never take an independent X rank.** Supersedes the pivot's "SCC quotienting / co-axial bidirectional hull pairs" — hull co-axiality is emergent from derived spans; leaf-level cycles are A3's job. Rejected: independent per-hull coordinate frames (round-3 backward-edge failure). *(pivot Core Rules 1–2; architecture-decision CORR-1/-2/-3; v3.1 §7 supersession line)*
- **SDEC-4 · rounds 2–5 · Cyclic policy = corrected GreedyFAS (ELS93) + E′ contract + styled back-edges; A3 runs before ranking.** No clamp fallback exists in the new engine (v1's inherited clamp = the +77% width blowout, measured round 6; shipped-v2 keeps it — won't-fix, superseded). Provenance correction: greedy FAS is ELK's default, NOT graphviz/dagre's. *(v2.0 A3/D5′; round-6 F10/F11; v3.1 §7)*
- **SDEC-5 · rounds 3–4 + round 6 · Hull-Y coordination is a NO-GO (twice measured).** Moving whole hulls' Y to shorten cross-container edges fails because the feasible region is intrinsically 2D-inseparable (frames are subtree bboxes). Round 6: the NO-GO transfers to A7's LCA-level block nudge — A7 is a slice-A refiner only. *(rcll-y-axis-hull-coord-nogo campaigns; round-6 F1)*
