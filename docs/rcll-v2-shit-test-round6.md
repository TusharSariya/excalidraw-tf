## 0. TL;DR

Round 6 asked three questions. All three have answers now.

1. **"Does my liked view generalize?" — NO, measured.** On the sparse preset (`staging-localstack`, 69 TFD arrows) the owner's exact config loses to the plain v2 baseline on *every* metric: median deviation 2,017px vs 459 (4.4× worse; only 1.7× on the canonical preset), crossings 124 vs 33, height 3.5× taller, and its rendering costs **18s of main-thread wall-clock**. The same battery gives the v2 substrate its second-preset validation (round 5's biggest caveat substantially reduced — two distinct graphs now, still not a battery: v2 compact wins both), and falsifies round 5's "rcll is ~4× less add-churn" (preset-specific: identical 32/70 moved on preset #2). What the owner likes about arm E is its *organization* (hub centering, containment, ancillary bands) — not its geometry, which is bad everywhere and worse off-preset.
2. **"Is v2 building the right thing?" — the backbone yes, the M1b payload no.** Three independent lanes + the fable refute panel converge: an edge participates in A2's ordering only at its endpoints' **LCA hull**; below that, every level orders blind to it; A0's banded stacks fix cross-band Y-separation; A7 only refines *inside* hulls and its LCA-level block-nudge is the move the hull-Y campaign already killed twice. Near-straight (≤24px) is **unreachable** for cross-band edges under this architecture — and it is empirically pinned at 0.10–0.17 (dense) and **0.00–0.04 (sparse)** across all 14 measured arms. A7 will likely *pass its own gate* on within-hull crumbs while the owner's complaint stays untouched, because the gate certifies the tail-blind **median** while A7 optimizes the tail-sensitive Σ|Δy|. The spec's escalation ladder (OD-3B dummies → full BK) aims at the wrong failure mode — dummies never move endpoint Y.
3. **"Right papers, right readings?" — mostly, with one flagship error.** The reading list's #1 harvest claim ("Forster 2004 is NOT in the corpus") is **false** — it's present with full text, and it happens to be the paper whose constrained-crossing-reduction method is the strongest candidate fix for the cross-hull ordering problem above. A3's correction is verbatim-exact against the JGAA restatement (attack failed); A4's citation is now faithful but the treemap→sparse adaptation has two real defects; A6's constants are right but the tombstone design contradicts C3′ against the real 24-hour wall-clock GC in the collab sync path.

**Posture correction that falls out:** the near-straightness lever for the owner's complaint is not A7 — it is (a) **A2's banded-hull ordering** (the same-layer barycenter the spec apologizes for is the load-bearing mechanism that brings connected bands adjacent), (b) **hub/ancillary extraction** (the TensorFlow-visualizer prerequisite, currently deferred to M3), and (c) **edge routing (OD-9)**, promoted from deferred to plan-of-record. A7 stays — as a cheap within-hull deviation reducer, gated by the statistic it actually optimizes.

## 2. Ranked findings

### CRITICAL

**F1 — The near-straightness thesis is structurally wrong: cross-hull vertical edge length is decided at A0/A2 band level and A7 cannot move it. The 2D-inseparability NO-GO transfers.** *(A7 lane + A0 lane + as-built trace + NO-GO memory + probes; composite verified by the fable refute panel)* `[R]`
Mechanics, panel-verified against spec text: each E′ edge participates in the **Y-ordering/Y-placement objective** at exactly **one** hull — its endpoints' LCA (spec §6-A2 lines 205–207 + A7 lines 271–273; each edge lifts once, Σ_h m_h = |E′|); below the LCA, the *internal* ordering/placement of every ancestor hull is blind to it (the edge still moves endpoint X everywhere via global A1 ranks — the corrected form) `[R: C1 CORRECTED-FORM]`. Cross-hull vertical edge length therefore decomposes into three terms with three different owners: **(t1) stacked band heights** — owned by packing density, no optimizer touches it; **(t2) bands-skipped × band height** — owned solely by A2's band order; **(t3) within-band endpoint position** — owned by *nothing* (blind below the LCA; A7 has zero freedom at banded LCAs — full-width gap-tight stacks — and only the twice-killed rigid-block nudge at packed ones, `rcll-y-axis-hull-coord-nogo`).
**The floor:** near-straight (≤24px vertical extent) is composition-floored for every cross-band edge at FRAME_PAD·2 + BAND_GAP + TITLE_RESERVE ≈ **208px under the as-built constants A0 normatively copies** (`terraformPipelineV2Pack.ts:70,75,95`) — 8.7× the threshold, independent of anything A2/A7 do `[R: C2 CORRECTED-FORM]`. The spec never pins these geometry constants ("any positive sibling gap") — C11 freezes metric thresholds but nothing freezes the geometry constants those thresholds' *reachability* depends on: a new defect in its own right. Empirically: near-straight is pinned 0.10–0.17 (preset 1) and 0.00–0.04 (preset 2) across **all fourteen** measured arms, exactly as the structural argument predicts.
**The panel's sharpest catch — the only lever is pointed at the wrong objective** `[R: C3 CORRECTED-FORM]`: A2's same-layer barycenter is the only *edge-aware, automatic* input to band order (model-order key and S6 user constraints are the other two channels) — but A2's acceptance gate admits a re-sorted sequence **only if crossings strictly decrease** (lines 229–231). A band reorder that brings connected bands adjacent (a multi-thousand-px t2 win) while leaving crossings flat is *rejected*. **As specified, the spec contains no mechanism whose accepted objective reduces cross-band edge length.** Worse for M1a: it ships at K=0 (line 402) — band order = pure content-key sort, i.e. the alphabetical ordering the whole A2 section exists to escape.
**The mis-aimed ladder** `[R: C4 CONFIRMED]`: OD-3B dummies and OD-5-2 BK cannot touch the cross-band class — rendered edges are 2-point chords (extent = endpoint |Δy|; any future waypoints only *increase* extent), same-layer band edges skip no columns so OD-3B generates no dummies for exactly these edges, and BK is band-clamped by A7's own constraint (b). Both remain legitimate for intra-packed straightness.
**Panel calibration:** P(A7-as-specified materially improves the owner's complaint) ≈ **0.05–0.10** `[R: C5]`.
**Fix (normative for v3, panel-designed):** (i) T9 scope-splits every E′ edge by **LCA-hull policy**: slice A = intra-packed (LCA ∈ region/vpc/subnetZone), slice B = cross-band (LCA ∈ account/provider). (ii) The owner-complaint gate becomes **p50/p90 vertical extent on slice B** + **mean bands-skipped per slice-B edge** (the statistic A2's band order actually moves) + **total stacked band height** (t1's owner metric), vs the v2 baseline, ≥2 presets. (iii) near-straight gates **slice A only**; on slice B it is report-only (gating on it gates the composition, not the code). (iv) **A2's banded-level acceptance is re-aimed**: accept reorders on strict decrease of Σ slice-B vertical extent (crossings as tiebreak/diagnostic) — or add it as a gated second acceptance arm. (v) SA7's gate = slice-A tail deviation (what A7 optimizes and owns). (vi) Geometry constants (FRAME_PAD/BAND_GAP/TITLE_RESERVE) frozen by amendment like thresholds (C11 extension). (vii) OD-9 routing promoted to plan-of-record for the flow-reading ceiling (owner decision before M2); hub/ancillary extraction pulled forward (F9f).

**F2 — The gate family certifies the wrong statistics: median is tail-blind, the owner's complaint IS the tail, and A7 is gated by a different quantity than it optimizes.** *(metrics lane + A4 lane + probes; code-anchored)*
(a) R4/T9 gate **median** vertical deviation (`terraformPipelineCollisionDiagnostics.ts:369-370`); a layout with 49% catastrophically-vertical edges leaves the median untouched. The owner's long-vertical-edge complaint is the upper tail. A7's own acceptance objective is **Σ|Δy|** (spec lines 253/265) — tail-sensitive — so the pass minimizes one statistic and is certified by another. `meanVerticalDeviationPx` is *already computed and ungated* (`:371-373`); round-6 probes now report it: the owner's arm E is tail-**worse** than the v2 baseline on both presets (mean 2,688 vs 1,589 on preset 1; 2,706 vs 1,247 on preset 2). (b) near-straight is a **binary 24px cliff** (`NEAR_STRAIGHT_MAX_PX`, `:374`): 23px scores like flat, 25px like 900px; bankable-by-optimizer, unstable near the cliff (an R7 hazard), and blind to how-bad. (c) crossings — the one metric the fooling literature (arXiv 2508.15557, deep-read) found robust — is demoted to a diagnostic; the fooling attack itself **fails** against this family (rank-pinning + hull-contiguity remove the 2D freedom the paper's morphs need — attack failed, stated plainly), but the paper's prescription (pair readability metrics with a robust structural one) is met only implicitly. (d) There is **no compactness metric at all** — "compact but never semantics-breaking" is priority #1 in the owner's own formulation, and aspect/W×H are shape, not density. (e) The OD-1 NS A/B is miscalibrated: network simplex minimizes the **symmetric** rank-span objective (Gansner TSE93 §2 — the axis the owner declared cheap); its −8.4% width win can mask NS-induced vertical stacking that the median gate cannot see (§4 D2 row: deviation 496→532 *rose* under NS while width improved).
**Fix (normative for v3):** gate on {median AND mean/p95} vertical deviation, split by within-hull/cross-hull scope (F1); add rank-span-normalized deviation (deviation ÷ ideal chord) as the honest asymmetric-objective formalization; add an area-utilization compactness metric; NS ships only if width AND tail-deviation both pass. Literature status of the asymmetric objective itself: **defensible, not established** — Helmke GD'24 (domain conventions override classical aesthetics, verified at source) and geodesic-path tendency support it; no study isolates vertical-vs-horizontal edge cost. It is an owner-calibrated engineering prior, and that is fine — say so in the spec instead of implying literature backing.

**F5 — A sixth silent seam: the export layer's provider re-stack is the sole owner of provider Y and the spec never names it.** *(orchestrator code verification + as-built trace)*
`applyCompoundHierarchicalLayout` re-stacks every provider subtree sequentially (`terraformPipelineLayoutCompoundHierarchy.ts:213-249`, `providerY += providerHeight + PIPELINE_LANE_GAP_Y`) and is called by **all three** existing export paths, including the v2 substrate rcll-v2 builds on (`terraformPipelineLayoutV2.ts:121`, `terraformPipelineLayoutRcll.ts:335`, `terraformPipelineLayoutCompound.ts:112`); `terraformPipelineRcllPlacement.ts:277` documents it as "the sole owner of provider Y." If rcll-v2's S0b reuses the substrate's scene build, A0's root-level placement is silently overwritten — single-provider presets mask it (pure translate); multi-provider breaks A0's root ordering and invalidates A7's re-anchor. **Fix:** C6′ seam #6 + an S0b acceptance assert (engine-emitted provider Y survives to the scene).

**F6 — A0 is not Sander-grounded at the placement level; per-hull bottom-up recursion is the configuration Forster GD'02 documents as producing unnecessary crossings; and the corpus contains the constrained-global-sweep fix the spec never cites.** *(A0 lane + A2 lane, both at source)*
Sander 96's own intro rejects the "recursively treat subgraphs as large nodes, ignore global connectivity" shape that A0 implements; Sander ranks over the extended nesting graph, gives spanning clusters **per-layer** positions (P̄ᵢ(u)), keeps border nodes and routing corridors — all dropped by A0 (accurate grounding: ranking/spans only). Forster GD'02 (p.278): independent interior/exterior ordering "leads to unnecessary edge crossings… no matter whether the hierarchy is traversed top down or bottom up," and recommends the **constraint method** — a single global two-layer sweep with contiguity constraints — implemented by **Forster 2004** (merge-on-violation constrained barycenter, <1% from optimal, `O(|V|log|V|+|E|+|C|²)`), which is IN the corpus (`forward-10-1007-978-3-540-31843-9-22`) despite the reading list's claim. A2's per-hull recursion **keeps the R2 contiguity guarantee** (that attack failed — same guarantee as the constraint method) but forfeits documented crossing quality, and two of its mechanisms are nonstandard: same-layer barycenter terms (ELK dummy-chains in-layer edges instead) and `colSpan.min` as a spanning unit's single layer (vs Sander's per-layer positions). **Disposition for v3:** keep hull-scoped A2 for M1 (structural contiguity + determinism are worth it), but (i) name Forster 04 as the designated A2 upgrade (OD-new: descendant-weighted constrained global sweep — stateless, deterministic, directly compatible with R7), (ii) acknowledge the same-layer term as load-bearing (F1), with a fixture asserting band adjacency follows connectivity.

**F7 — A2's acceptance test is underspecified where it matters and safe where round 6 expected it to be broken.** *(A2 lane; cost attack + probes)*
The cost attack **failed**: per-hull scoping bounds the trial-placement + crossing-count sweep at ~4–160ms even in pathological concentration (Σ_h m_h = |E′|; probes: full-scene crossing diagnostics run in 1–3ms at 145 arrows) — comfortably inside OD-10. Three real defects: (i) the acceptance clause's "full deterministic crossing count over lifted E′" reads globally 24 lines away from its per-hull scoping — the misread costs ~100× and blows the budget; (ii) the **chord attachment geometry is undefined** (unit boxes: center? representative leaf? port?) — the gate optimizes a proxy that can diverge from the leaf-level T9 metric it ships against; (iii) per-sweep strict-improvement acceptance can **freeze the alphabetical start** (barycenter needs transient uphill moves; Sander/Forster iterate to convergence and keep the best) — the failure mode is a silently ONO-adjacent order, the exact thing A2 exists to prevent. Also: A2 is honestly "Strategy-1-inspired," not Strategy 1 (GD'24's mechanism is run-crossing-min-then-select-by-model-order; A2's gate is a third thing) — relabel, don't re-cite. **Fix:** pin the scoping words + attachment-point definition (recommend: unit box center, stated); replace per-sweep strict-improvement with best-of-K-sweeps acceptance (still deterministic, still bounded); keep K=4 as OD-2.

**F8 — A4's treemap→node-link adaptation has two landing defects the round-5 correction did not consider.** *(A4 lane at source + arithmetic)*
Sondag's measure is citation-faithful now (Eq 1–2/Fig 17–19/translation-invariance all verified — that attack failed), but: (i) in sparse layouts distant small boxes are one-hot in a single sector — crossing an extended-side line flips the pair's full mass (D_rel 0→1) on a tiny move; the paper never distance-weights because treemaps tile; (ii) the |U|²-pair **mean** washes out localized big moves — m=2 movers among n=400 cap M1_rel at ~0.01, inside the brittleness noise floor, while M3 p95 (which spikes on exactly this) is explicitly ungated, and M2_flip sees only within-column inversions. **Fix (v3):** gate M1_rel + M2_flip as specified *plus* a per-node displacement p95 over U (translation-corrected by the column-median shift — this respects the Sondag argument while catching localized churn); add Meidiana-style cluster-membership stability (ARI over hull membership — O(n), non-brittle, node-link-validated) as the structural complement.

**F9 — v1 issues dropped without disposition (the traceability audit's residue).** *(inventory agents + spec cross-check; full matrix §5)*
(a) **DEC-7 huge fan-out**: unimplemented in v1 (`hugeFanoutThreshold` accepted-but-unread), absent from v2 entirely; the modern answer exists — ELK 0.10's **layer unzipping**. (b) **T4 fan-out column pinning** and (c) **T5 hub centering**: v1 phase objectives, demoted to unowned metrics in v2 — and hub centering is the one metric where the owner's preferred arm actually won on preset 1 (0.11) while M1's gates never compare against arm E at all; an rcll-v2 M1 can pass every gate and still lose to the view the owner already has. (d) **Failure-mode posture**: v1 had a 5-rung degradation ladder + `rcllDegraded` observability; v2 has dev-asserts and "deliberately no clamp fallback" with no stated runtime behavior on pathological input. (e) **Q7-AXIS** (does X = dependency depth match the direction users read as dataflow?) — flagged in round-5 F17, still unscheduled in v2.0. (f) **Extraction timing**: the TF-visualizer paper (closest production analog) treats high-degree/auxiliary extraction as a legibility **prerequisite** (their Step 1/3 before layout); v2 defers ancillary to M3 and has no hub-extraction concept, so M1's readability battery will measure exactly the intertwined view the owner's ancillary toggle exists to avoid. **Fix:** v3 dispositions each — (a) OD-new: layer-unzip A/B post-M1; (b/c) fan-out alignment + hub centering into T9 as **arm-E-comparative** report rows, with an explicit M-milestone owner; (d) a minimal engine-failure contract (assert → structured `rcllDegraded`-style meta + refuse-to-ship-silently); (e) Q7-AXIS scheduled as a 20-edge hand-label study before M2 gates freeze; (f) ancillary/extraction pulled to M1-adjacent (at minimum: the M1 battery runs WITH ancillary strips on, since every real view the owner uses has them).

## 4. The mechanics (what actually produces the owner's long vertical edges)

### 4.1 As-built trace of the liked config (P0)

Ranked structural sources, with the pass that *should* fix each and why it can't:

1. **Provider/account forced-band sequential stacking** (`placeForcedBands`, plain `cursorY` accumulation, never consults `riseStackY`) — an edge from an early band to a late band spans every intervening band's full height. No existing pass has reach here: `straighten` is per-container ±1-column; `coordRepack` never grows/moves bands; `swimlaneRise`/`staircase` only fire inside cyclic subtrees; `rankSeparate` only rewrites X.

3. **Cyclic-container swimlane stacks** — all-or-nothing X-disjointness gate; one shared column forces full sequential stacking.
4. **No dummy chains** — multi-column edges have no node to chain through (`straightenColumns` docstring: the "spine tail").

Only observable request-vs-executed divergence in the liked config: `reorder` silently dropped (crossingMin wins) — and the probe meta now echoes `pipelineNetworkSimplexRankSuppressed=true` (the C6′ discipline working).
